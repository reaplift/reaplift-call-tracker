# How long Callit keeps data

**DRAFT for [Client company name] to decide, with its legal team. The "Proposed" column is ReapLift's suggestion. Once agreed, these periods become part of the Callit Service Agreement and ReapLift sets Callit to delete data automatically.**

| Data | What it is | Today | Proposed | Decided |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Call recordings | Audio of company-SIM calls | Deleted after 12 months | 12 months | [ ] |
| Call details | Number, contact name, type, time, durations | Kept with no limit yet | 24 months | [ ] |
| After-call notes | Outcome, comment, follow-up date | Kept with the call | Same as call details | [ ] |
| Data of employees who leave | Their calls and recordings | Kept | Calls: 12 months after leaving. Recordings: their normal 12 months | [ ] |
| Phone details | Brand, model, app version | Kept while the phone is paired | Deleted 30 days after a phone is unpaired | [ ] |
| Logs of who played a recording | Who played which call, when | Kept | 24 months | [ ] |
| Panel logins | Names and emails of managers and admins | Until removed | Removed when the person leaves | [ ] |
| Numbers on the never-record list | Numbers whose calls aren't kept | Until removed | Until an admin removes them | [ ] |
| All data when the Company stops using Callit | Everything above | No rule yet | Exportable for 30 days, then deleted; backups within 60 days | [ ] |

Notes:

- Calls with numbers on the never-record list are not stored at all.
- If a customer or employee asks for their data to be deleted, an admin can do it straight away using the never-record list's "delete past calls" option.
- Some laws or contracts may require keeping records for longer, for example for tax or disputes. Please tell us if that applies.
