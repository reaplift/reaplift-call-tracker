# Company SIM and Call Tracking Policy

**DRAFT for [Client company name]'s legal team or HR. ReapLift prepared this as a starting point. Not legal advice. Text in [square brackets] is to be filled in or decided.**

Issued by [Client company name] ("the Company"), effective [date].

## 1. Why this policy exists

The Company gives some employees a company SIM card for work calls with customers. To improve customer service and follow up on enquiries, the Company keeps a record of those calls using an app called **Callit**, provided by ReapLift Private Limited.

## 2. Who this policy applies to

Every employee who is given a company SIM card.

## 3. Using the company SIM

3.1 The company SIM is for **work calls only**. Please use your own SIM for personal calls.

3.2 Don't give the company SIM to anyone else, and tell your manager straight away if it is lost or stolen.

3.3 When you leave the Company, return the company SIM to [person or team].

## 4. What Callit records

For calls made or received **on your company SIM only**, Callit sends to the Company:

- the other person's number and the name saved for them on your phone;
- whether the call was incoming, outgoing, missed or rejected;
- the date and time, how long it rang and how long you talked;
- the recording of the call, if your phone's calling app records calls;
- the outcome, comment and follow-up date you add after the call.

## 5. What Callit never records

- Calls on your **personal SIM**. They never leave your phone.
- Your messages, contacts list, photos, location, or other apps.

## 6. Who can see your call records

- Your manager, for the teams they manage.
- The Company's Callit administrators: [names or roles].
- ReapLift, which runs Callit for the Company, only to keep the service working.
- The Company's CRM.

You can see your own company-SIM calls in the Callit app.

## 7. How long records are kept

- Call recordings: [12 months], then deleted.
- Other call details: [period].
- After you leave the Company: [period], then deleted.

## 8. Calling customers

8.1 At the start of each call, tell the customer: *"[This call is recorded for quality purposes.]"*

8.2 If a customer doesn't want to be recorded, [tell your manager, who can stop recording that number].

## 9. Your choices and questions

9.1 You can ask to see the call records Callit holds about you, or ask for a mistake to be corrected, by contacting [person or email].

9.2 If you no longer agree to call tracking, tell [person]. [What happens: for example, the Company will discuss other work arrangements with you.]

9.3 Questions about this policy: [person or email].

## 10. Breaking this policy

Misusing the company SIM, or trying to stop Callit from working on a company phone, may lead to [disciplinary action under the Company's rules].
