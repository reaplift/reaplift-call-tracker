# Callit Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

## 1. Who we are

Callit is a work app provided by **ReapLift Private Limited**, [registered address], India (**"ReapLift"**, **"we"**). Companies use Callit to keep a record of their employees' business calls on company SIM cards.

Callit is not for personal use. It is set up by an employer for its own employees.

**Who is responsible for your data:** the company you work for (or the company you spoke with, if you are a customer) decides how your call data is used. ReapLift stores and handles the data for that company, only as it instructs. For questions about your data, contact that company first. You can also contact us at [email].

## 2. What the app collects

From the employee's work phone, **for calls on the company SIM only**:

- the other person's phone number, and the name saved for them on the phone;
- whether the call was incoming, outgoing, missed or rejected;
- the date and time, how long it rang and how long it lasted;
- the call recording, if the phone's own calling app recorded it;
- the outcome, comment and follow-up date the employee adds;
- basic details about the phone: brand, model, Android version, app version, and which SIM slot is the company SIM.

From people who log in to the Callit website: name, email address, and a log of their logins and of each time they played a recording.

## 3. What the app does not collect

- Calls on a personal SIM. The app only checks which SIM a call used; personal-SIM calls never leave the phone.
- Text messages, the contacts list, photos, location, or other apps.
- Audio files other than the recording of a company-SIM call. To find that recording, the app looks at the names, times and lengths of audio files saved around the time of the call; it opens and uploads only the one that matches.
- Calls with numbers the employer has put on its never-record list.

## 4. Why we use it

Only to provide Callit to the employer: to show its managers call history, reports and recordings, to let its CRM read them, and to help the employee follow up with customers. We don't sell the data, use it for advertising, or use it to train software.

## 5. Who can see it

- The employer's managers and Callit administrators.
- The employer's CRM, if the employer connects it.
- ReapLift staff who need it to run and support Callit.
- The companies we use to host Callit: Hostinger (server and database, India) and Cloudflare (recordings, [location]). They store the data for us and can't use it for anything else.

## 6. How we keep it safe

All connections are encrypted. Passwords and access keys are stored in a scrambled form. Each employer's data is kept separate. Recordings can only be played through links that expire within minutes, and every play is logged.

## 7. How long we keep it

Recordings are deleted after [12 months]. Other call details are kept for [period], or as the employer decides. When an employer stops using Callit, its data is deleted within [60] days.

## 8. Your choices

Employees and customers can ask the employer to see, correct or delete their call records, or not to record their calls. We help the employer answer these requests. Employees can stop tracking by asking their employer to disconnect their phone, or by uninstalling the app.

## 9. Children

Callit is a work tool for employees. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell employers. If a change affects what the app collects, the app asks employees to agree again.
