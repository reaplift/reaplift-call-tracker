## 1. What this agreement is about

1.1 ReapLift provides **Callit**, a service that keeps a record of the business calls of the Company's employees on company SIM cards (and the recordings their phones make), shows them to the Company's managers, and makes them available to the Company's CRM.

1.2 Callit is made up of an Android app installed on employees' work phones, a website for the Company's managers, and an interface the Company's CRM can connect to.

1.3 The Company decides why call data is collected, which employees use Callit, who in the Company can see the data, and how long it is kept. ReapLift handles the data only to provide Callit to the Company, and only as this agreement allows.

## 2. What ReapLift will do

2.1 Provide Callit for the employees the Company adds, as described in Schedule A.

2.2 Keep Callit available on working days, aiming for [99]% availability each month, apart from planned maintenance announced [24] hours in advance.

2.3 Fix faults the Company reports: urgent faults (no calls arriving, nobody can log in) within [1 working day]; other faults within [5 working days].

2.4 Tell the Company before making a change that affects what data Callit collects.

## 3. What the Company will do

3.1 Use Callit only for its employees' business calls on company SIM cards.

3.2 Before an employee starts using Callit: give them the Company SIM Policy, get their signed acknowledgement, and make sure they accept the message the app shows before tracking starts.

3.3 Tell its customers that calls may be recorded, as agreed with its own legal team.

3.4 Keep its Callit logins and API keys safe, and remove access for people who leave.

3.5 Pay the fees in Schedule C.

## 4. How ReapLift handles the Company's data

4.1 **Only on the Company's instructions.** ReapLift uses the data only to provide Callit to the Company. ReapLift will not sell it, use it for advertising, use it to train software, or share it with anyone not listed in Schedule B.

4.2 **Kept separate.** Each client company's data is kept separate. One company can never see another company's data.

4.3 **Kept safe.** ReapLift keeps the data safe as described in Schedule B, including: encrypted connections, passwords and API keys stored in a form that can't be read back, recording links that stop working after a few minutes, and a log of every time someone plays a recording.

4.4 **People at ReapLift.** Only ReapLift staff who need it to run Callit can reach the data, and they are bound to keep it confidential.

4.5 **Other companies ReapLift uses.** ReapLift uses the companies listed in Schedule B to host Callit. ReapLift will tell the Company [30] days before adding or changing one, and the Company may object.

4.6 **Where the data is kept.** As listed in Schedule B. ReapLift will not move the data to another country without telling the Company first [and getting its agreement].

4.7 **Data leaks.** If ReapLift finds that the Company's data has been lost, stolen or seen by someone who shouldn't have seen it, ReapLift will tell the Company within [24] hours of finding out, explain what happened and what data was affected, and help the Company meet its own duty to inform the authorities and the people affected.

4.8 **Requests from people.** If an employee or customer asks ReapLift to see, correct or delete their data, ReapLift will pass the request to the Company within [3 working days] and help the Company answer it.

4.9 **Checks.** Once a year, or after a data leak, the Company may ask for a written description of how ReapLift keeps the data safe, and may ask reasonable questions about it.

## 5. How long data is kept

5.1 Data is kept for the periods in the Retention Schedule agreed by the Company, and deleted automatically after that.

5.2 Calls with numbers on the Company's never-record list are not stored. When the Company adds such a number and chooses to delete earlier calls, ReapLift deletes them and their recordings.

## Schedule A: What Callit does

- The app runs on the employee's work phone. It sends to ReapLift's server **only calls made or received on the company SIM**: the other person's number, the contact name saved on the phone, whether the call was incoming, outgoing, missed or rejected, the date and time, and how long it rang and lasted.
- Calls on a personal SIM are never read beyond telling which SIM they used, and never leave the phone.
- Callit does not record calls. If the phone's own calling app records calls, Callit looks at the list of audio files saved around the time of each company-SIM call, opens only the one that matches, and uploads it. Other audio files are never opened.
- After a call, the employee can add an outcome (for example "Interested"), a comment and a follow-up date.
- The app also sends basic details about the phone (brand, model, Android version, app version, which SIM slot is the company SIM) so ReapLift can support it.
- Managers see calls, reports, dashboards and recordings on the Callit website. Each manager sees only their own teams.
- The Company's CRM can read the same data with an API key the Company's admin creates.
- Admins can list numbers that are never recorded or stored, and delete earlier calls with those numbers.
- Planned: employees send ready-made WhatsApp messages from their own WhatsApp.

**How data is kept safe:**

- All connections between phones, browsers, the CRM and Callit are encrypted (HTTPS).
- Passwords and API keys are stored only in a scrambled form that can't be turned back into the original.
- Each login has a role (admin or manager), and managers see only their own teams.
- Recordings are private. A recording can only be played through a link that stops working after 5 minutes (24 hours for the CRM), and every play is logged.
- Repeated wrong passwords lock the login.
- The database is backed up [daily] and backups are kept for [7 days].
