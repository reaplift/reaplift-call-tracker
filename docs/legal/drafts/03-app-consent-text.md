# Message the Callit app shows before tracking starts

**DRAFT for [Client company name]'s legal team. ReapLift prepared this. Not legal advice.**

This is the screen each employee sees when setting up Callit. Tracking only starts after they tap **I agree**. The app keeps a record of the date, time and version of the text each employee agreed to. If the text changes, the app asks again before tracking continues.

The text below is based on what the beta app shows today (version 2), with the Company's name and the 12-month recording period added. Proposed as **version 3**.

---

**Before you start: what Callit does**

[Client company name] uses Callit to keep a record of work calls on your company SIM. Callit is run for the Company by ReapLift.

**What is sent from this phone**

- Calls on your **company SIM only**: the number, the contact name saved on your phone, the date and time, whether it was incoming, outgoing, missed or rejected, how long you talked and how long it rang.
- The **recordings** of those company-SIM calls, made by your phone's own calling app. Only the recording that matches a company-SIM call is opened and sent. Other audio files are never opened.
- The outcome, comment and follow-up date you add after a call.

**What is never sent**

- Calls on your **personal SIM**. They never leave your phone.
- Your messages, contacts list, photos, location or other apps.

**Who sees it**

Your managers, the Company's Callit administrators, and ReapLift to keep the service working. You can see everything that was sent in this app.

**How long it is kept**

Recordings are deleted after 12 months. [Other details are kept for ____.]

**Your choices**

You can ask [person or email] to stop tracking at any time; they will disconnect this phone. Uninstalling the app also stops it. Read the Company SIM Policy for more.
