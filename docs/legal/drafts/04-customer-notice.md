# Telling customers that calls are recorded

**DRAFT for [Client company name]'s legal team. ReapLift prepared this as a starting point. Not legal advice.**

## 1. What the employee says at the start of each call

> "Hello, this is [name] from [Client company name]. This call is recorded for quality purposes."

[Decide: on every call, or only the first call with each customer.]

The phones' calling apps don't play an automatic announcement, so the employee says it. When the planned "call from the Callit app" feature is built, the app will show this line on screen before each call as a reminder.

## 2. Paragraph for the Company's website privacy page

> **Phone calls.** When you speak with our team by phone, we record the call and keep a note of it, including your phone number, the date and time, how long the call lasted, and any follow-up our team plans. We use this to serve you better, to follow up on your enquiry, and to train our team. Recordings are kept for [12 months] and then deleted. Our call records are stored for us by our service provider, ReapLift Private Limited, in [India]. To see or delete your call records, or to ask us not to record your calls, contact [email or phone].

## 3. If a customer asks not to be recorded, or to delete their calls

1. The employee tells [person or team].
2. A Callit admin adds the customer's number to **Settings → Never record these numbers**. From then on, calls with that number are not stored or recorded.
3. If the customer asked for deletion, the admin ticks **"Also delete past calls and recordings with this number"** when adding it.
4. [Person] replies to the customer within [time].
