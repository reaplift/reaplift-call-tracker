Two requests from Country Roads, drawn from the Callyzer screens they shared. Nothing is built yet. Please approve the screens and answer the questions at the end of each part. Building starts after that.

1. Dashboard: the first page after login

Three cards (Today, Yesterday, Last week) with the same 13 numbers Callyzer shows. Click any number to see the calls behind it. Managers see only their teams. The numbers come from the same code as the CRM API, so the panel and the CRM always agree.

2. WhatsApp message templates

The admin writes a few fixed messages. After a call, the employee picks one; the client's name is filled in from the phone's contacts, or the employee types it. They check the message, and WhatsApp opens on the client's chat with the text ready. The employee presses WhatsApp's send button. The panel records which template went to which client.

To decide

W1

Who writes templates? You wrote "super admin". In Callit, super admin is ReapLift; each company also has its own admins.

Proposed: each company's admins write their own; ReapLift can do it for any company; employees can't.

W2

Send from the employee's WhatsApp, or from one company number through Meta's API?

Proposed: the employee's WhatsApp, as compared above.

W3

Should employees use WhatsApp Business registered on the company SIM, so clients see the company number?

Proposed: the app opens WhatsApp Business when installed, otherwise WhatsApp. The company tells employees which to use.

W4

Can the employee change the wording, or only fill the blanks?

Proposed: only the blanks (name, date). The wording stays as the company wrote it.

W5

Any other blanks needed, such as product, site or price?

Proposed: client name, employee name, company name and follow-up date for now.

W6

Start every company with Welcome, Follow-up and "Not interested (keep in touch)"?

Proposed: yes, editable.
