# M8. Pilot

- Status: Not started
- Owner: ReapLift
- Starts after: M3, M5, M6, M7
- External prerequisites: E1 to E6

## Outcome

Five employees from one team use the app for two weeks on the live server, then everyone. Managers use the panel and the CRM pages daily. The pilot is done when two weeks pass with no call missing from the history that the phone's own call log shows, recording coverage at or above the target, and no employee needing help after setup.

## Plan

1. **Week 0:** phone survey (E2) done; phones that cannot record replaced or listed as "no recordings". Consent text approved (Q4).
2. **Weeks 1 and 2:** five employees. Every morning: the daily email checked against two employees' phone call logs by hand; the Phones page reviewed; any problem fixed and recorded.
3. **Weeks 3 and 4:** all employees, installed in groups of 10, with the setup time recorded for each.

## Measures

| Measure | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Company-SIM calls in our history ÷ calls in the phone's call log (spot checks) | 100% |
| Personal-SIM calls uploaded | 0 |
| Recording coverage (recorded ÷ expected) on supported phones | 95% or more |
| Phones red for more than a working day | none |
| Setup time without help | under 5 minutes |

## Demo (milestone acceptance)

The measures for the last two weeks, with the spot-check evidence, in `e2e.md`, and the owner's sign-off.
